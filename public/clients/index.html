How each project is presented

Every engagement follows the same layout, so clients can be compared at a glance. Fields still waiting for confirmation are shown like this: TBC · country. Commercial figures are shown as $XXX.

FieldWhat it shows
ClientName, website, country and sector
Engagement typeAI Transformation, AI Strategy or AI Product Build. The first listed is the main one.
Company typeStartup, Small–mid size or Enterprise
One-linerWhat the client's business does, in one sentence
OverviewThe brief, written as: the client needed X to help them with Y
ServicesWhich Automagic services were used: Discovery, Design, Build in waves, Adopt, Run and evolve, Fractional Transformation Lead
TimelineStart date, duration, and the phases or waves in order
StructureThe commercial model: fixed cost per tool or agent, monthly consultancy, fractional lead, and who pays external costs
SolutionsWhat was delivered, named as the client knows it
Bespoke builds and IPEach custom asset, what kind of asset it is, and who owns the IP
OutcomesMeasured results, or the KPIs being tracked
