How to read a profile

Every project follows one template

Each client profile uses the same fields, so engagements can be compared at a glance.

01ClientName, website, country and sector
02Engagement typeAI Transformation, AI Strategy or AI Product Build. The first listed is the main one.
03Company typeStartup, Small–mid size or Enterprise
04One-linerWhat the client's business does, in one sentence
05OverviewThe brief: the client needed X to help them with Y
06ServicesDiscovery, Design, Build in waves, Adopt, Run and evolve, Fractional Transformation Lead
07TimelineStart date, duration, and the phases or waves in order
08StructureFixed cost per tool or agent, monthly consultancy, fractional lead, and who pays external costs
09SolutionsWhat was delivered, named as the client knows it
10Bespoke builds and IPEach custom asset, what kind of asset it is, and who owns the IP
11OutcomesMeasured results, or the KPIs being tracked
12StatusCompleted, Live or In progress
