Chapter 01 · Who we are

AI transformation built for complex, expert-driven businesses

Automagic AI helps knowledge-intensive businesses find where AI creates a real operational advantage, then builds it, embeds it and runs it. We are not a software house selling a product, and we are not a generalist consultancy selling time. We take responsibility for the outcome, from the first interview to tools that consultants, brokers or advisers use every day.

Our work goes deepest where value rests on expert judgement, relationship capital and complex information flows: executive search, specialty brokerage, financial and professional services.

Recent work

  • Hunter Executive. Discovery, roadmap and delivery of PESA, a Personal Executive Search Assistant for every consultant: a governed AI operating layer on top of the firm's CRM, with 12 agents in four suites covering lead intelligence, sourcing, relationship scoring, meeting intelligence, outreach and proposal generation, delivered in three waves.
  • Financial services. AI Transformation Discovery for firms with complex, multi-party transaction and advisory workflows, focused on cycle time, decision quality and client-facing differentiation.

Chapter 03 · Process

The five phases in detail

What happens in each phase, what you receive, and what we need from your team.

PHASE 01

Discover

3–4 days

A focused investigation that ends in a decision, not a pile of observations. We map how work flows today, where time and information are lost, and where AI creates the most leverage.

What we do

  • Structured interviews across leadership and front-line teams
  • Workflow mapping, stage by stage
  • Data and systems audit: what exists, where, in what form
  • Market and competitor research on AI adoption
  • Regulatory and data governance review

What you receive

  • AI Transformation Report: current state, opportunities, readiness
  • Prioritised roadmap by impact, effort and sequencing
  • Build, buy or configure view per opportunity
  • Leadership readout and Q&A

What we need

  • 60–90 minute interviews with key people
  • Representative workflow materials and templates
  • One coordinator to schedule access

View a real Stage 1 discovery report example

PHASE 02

Design

1–2 weeks

We turn the roadmap into something you can approve: a solution design, a scoped set of tools and changes, a delivery plan in waves, and a business case with the KPIs it will be judged on.

What we do

  • Solution design on top of your existing systems
  • Scoping of each tool, agent or change, with build estimates
  • Value against effort prioritisation into waves
  • Business case and ROI model with your figures
  • KPI definitions and baseline plan

What you receive

  • Findings and solution report
  • Proposal with a fixed price per item
  • Wave plan and success criteria
  • Risk, dependency and data protection plan

What we need

  • An executive sponsor and a product owner
  • Access to system owners for integration review
  • Input on business case assumptions

PHASE 03

Build in waves

TBD, set in Design

What we build, how many waves there are and how long they take are not known until discovery and design are done. What is fixed is how we deliver: in short waves, so your team uses real tools early, each wave building on the one before, and every item priced and agreed before its wave starts.

Each wave includes

  • Design, build and integration
  • Testing against agreed cases
  • Onboarding and launch
  • Tuning from real usage

You receive

  • Working tools inside your existing systems
  • Wave review: adoption, time saved, issues
  • Go or no-go for the next wave

We need

  • Subject-matter experts for each wave
  • Timely access to systems and data
  • Product owner sign-off at wave end

PHASE 04

Adopt

Alongside every wave

Tools that are built but not used return nothing. Adoption runs inside every wave, not after it.

What we do

  • A 90-minute hands-on session per team, per wave
  • Weekly office hours for four weeks after each launch
  • Champions in each team who see features a week early
  • Feedback captured inside the tools

What you receive

  • Trained, confident users
  • Usage and feedback reports each month
  • A backlog of improvements from real use

What we need

  • Time for training sessions
  • One champion per team
  • Leadership visibly using the tools

PHASE 05

Run and evolve

Ongoing

Once live, everything delivered is monitored, tuned and extended. The data and results it produces shape the next stage.

What we do

  • Monitoring, fixes and model and prompt tuning
  • Cost and usage control per tool
  • Monthly steering and quarterly ROI reviews
  • Scoping of the next stage

Where it leads

  • New opportunities found through real use
  • Scoping of the next stage, using the data and results from this one

What we need

  • A named product owner
  • Attendance at the monthly steering meeting

Chapter 04 · Principles

How we build, whatever we build

We don't decide what to build before discovery. The solution comes from what we find. These principles apply to everything we deliver.

Fit

Built on what you already run

We work with your existing systems and data rather than replacing them, unless discovery shows a clear reason to.

Evidence

Prioritised by value and readiness

Each item is scored on impact, effort and data readiness, so the order of work is a business decision, not a technical one.

Governance

Under your control

Clear rules for data, approvals and compliance, and human sign-off wherever your policies require it.

Ownership

Yours, in full

You own what we build, and you pay external providers directly with no mark-up.

Proof

Measured from day one

Baselines before launch and agreed KPIs after, so every wave shows what it returned.

Chapter 05 · Return on investment

Measuring the return

Every engagement is judged on four measures, agreed in Design and baselined before the first launch. Targets are set from your own figures, not industry averages.

Time

Hoursreturned

per person per week, measured by time sampling before launch and at each review

Efficiency

%faster

cycle times from system timestamps, such as lead to outreach or brief to proposal

Pipeline

+opportunities

new opportunities and mandates credited to AI signals, under an agreed attribution rule

Return

Monthsto payback

time value plus new revenue, against total cost, reviewed at agreed points

ROI = (value of time returned + revenue from attributed wins − total cost) ÷ total cost
